# Mercury Marine: Full History (1939-2026) Tied Into Offshore Powerboat Racing (TASK-017, Research Lane F)

_Research question: Mercury Marine's full company/engine history from 1939 to current, and its intertwined
racing lineage (Kiekhaefer Aeromarine -> Mercury Racing) plus the current state of offshore powerboat racing
engine rules._
_Compiled: 2026-07-10. Web research only — the local knowledge base (`knowledge/entries/*.md`,
`knowledge/graph/graph.json`) does not exist in this repo; the `knowledge/` directory here holds raw Grok-export
source documents rather than a lessons-learned KB, so the mandated KB-lookup step found nothing to score (see
Coverage notes). `knowledge/03_Offshore_Powerboat_Racing_Origins_and_History.md` and
`knowledge/04_Johnson_and_Evinrude_Motor_History.md` were read first; both barely mention Mercury (a passing
MerCruiser/Formula-233 reference and a Jim Wynne/Kiekhaefer-Mercury employment note), so there is no
duplication risk. Cross-references to those two files are called out inline below._
