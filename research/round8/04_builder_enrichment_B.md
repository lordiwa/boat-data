## Unresolved (could not ground with available sources)

- **Mixed** (`builder:mixed`) — This does not resemble a real shipyard name; it reads like a generic/placeholder category value ("mixed [materials/builders]") that may have been mis-ingested as a builder node. No company by this name was found. Recommend a data-quality check on the source document rather than further web research.
- **Motorsailer** (`builder:motorsailer`) — Same pattern as "Mixed": "motorsailer" is a recognized vessel *type* (a cruising yacht using both engine and sail as co-equal propulsion), not a company name. No shipyard called "Motorsailer" was found. Recommend a data-quality check.
- **Shear Yachts** (`builder:shear-yachts`) — Appears only as a bare name on aggregator/broker listing pages (Yacht Harbour, CharterWorld, Bluewater Yachting); no company profile, country, founding year, or website could be found on any source checked, including itBoat, Boat International, and SuperYacht Times searches. Do not confuse with "Shearline Boatworks" (Morehead City, NC, USA) — a different, unrelated company that surfaced repeatedly in searches for a similar name.
- **Stainless Structures** (`builder:stainless-structures`) — No yacht-builder profile found under this exact name on any shipyard directory checked. The only close match, "Stainless Structurals" (Jacksonville → Conroe, TX, founded 2005), is a stainless-steel structural-shapes manufacturer for architecture/industry, not a yacht builder — almost certainly a different, unrelated company, so not used.
- **Northstar Yachts** (`builder:northstar-yachts`) — NAME COLLISION, not grounded: (a) North Star Yachts Ltd., a Canadian fiberglass sailboat builder active 1969-1977 in Ontario (a rebranded U.S. Steel-owned spin-off of Hughes Boat Works), vs. (b) a modern "NorthStar Yachts" brand tracked by SuperYacht Times across "explorer", "sport boat", and "fast displacement" yacht categories, which multiple listings associate with Taiwan-built construction. With only 1 yacht attached to this graph node, I could not determine which era/company it refers to, so all cells are left blank rather than guessed.
- **Xplorer** (`builder:xplorer`) — Possible misattribution: "Xplorer" is used by at least two different entities in the industry — (a) Damen Yachting's "Xplorer" expedition-yacht model line (60/80/105, Netherlands), where Damen itself, not "Xplorer," is the legal builder; and (b) "Xplorer Yachts," a separate expedition-yacht specialist/brokerage that in 2026 contracted Turkish "Norse Shipyard" to build a 106m wind-assisted explorer (Project MG) — i.e., a company that commissions builds rather than building in-house. Neither cleanly matches "Xplorer" as a standalone shipyard, so left blank.
- **Odisej founding year** and **Mie Zosen founding year/website** — the companies themselves are grounded (see table), but no source gave a foundation year or (for Mie Zosen) any website.
- **Rauma, Mayra Yachts, Platinum Marine, Van der Graaf, Warren Yachts, Yachtsourcing, Pacific Motor Boat founding years** — company/country grounded but no bare 4-digit founding year could be sourced; see per-row notes for what was found (e.g., "early 1980s," "1980s") that fell short of the bare-year requirement.
