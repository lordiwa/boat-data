#### 1. The True History of Yacht Insurance (from ancient roots to 2026)
Marine (and later yacht) insurance is the **oldest form of commercial insurance** in the world.

### Yachts > Legal & Regulations > Marine Salvage Laws > Core Framework (1989 Salvage Convention — the global backbone)
The **International Convention on Salvage 1989** (adopted London 28 April 1989, in force 14 July 1996) is the primary international treaty. The US treats it as self-executing supreme federal law. **Key Definitions (Article 1)**
- Salvage operation: any act/activity to assist a vessel or other property in danger (navigable waters or any waters).
- Vessel: any ship/craft or structure capable of navigation (explicitly includes **yachts**, pleasure craft, superyachts).
- Property: anything not permanently attached to shoreline (includes freight, tenders, gear).

**Major Innovations (why it matters for yachts)**

- Duties on all parties (Article 8): Master/owner must cooperate; salvor must use best efforts and prevent environmental damage.
- Security & arrest: Salved property cannot be removed from first port without salvor consent until security posted.
- Time limit: 2 years to bring claims (Article 23).
