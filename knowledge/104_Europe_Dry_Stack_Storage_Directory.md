## Overview

Yacht dry-stack storage in Europe refers to on-shore, rack-based or shed-based storage systems for pleasure craft, ranging from small sports boats and RIBs to mid-size motor yachts. The model has grown rapidly since the 2010s as marinas face space constraints, environmental regulation, and owner demand for lower-maintenance, cost-effective alternatives to traditional wet berths.

Europe's dry-stack landscape is characterized by:

- Large single-site indoor facilities (Trafalgar Wharf in the UK, Port Fòrum in Spain)
- Multi-country networks standardizing operations (Port Adhoc Group in France, the Netherlands, and Sweden)
- Specialized indoor warehouses for yachts and tenders in major refit hubs (Berthon Spain in Palma de Mallorca)

## Storage Models and Typology

1. **High-density indoor racked dry stack.** Multi-level steel racks inside large sheds; boats on cradles or trailers moved by marine forklifts; typical for sports boats, RIBs, and mid-size cruisers up to about 13 m (43 ft). Examples: Trafalgar Wharf, Port Fòrum, many Port Adhoc sites. Advantages: maximizes boats per square meter of waterfront, reduces hull fouling and antifouling use, often cheaper than equivalent wet berths in congested harbors.
2. **Single-story covered sheds or hangars.** Clear-span warehouses with boats on hardstand or low racks; common in Mediterranean wintering yards and refit centers; suitable for yachts up to 15–20 m. Example: Berthon Spain's 2,000 m² warehouse in Palma.
3. **Hardstand cradle storage (open or partially covered).** Outdoor cradle storage, sometimes under partial cover; widely used for seasonal wintering in the Mediterranean; often paired with shrink-wrap and winterization. Operated by large groups such as Metropol Náuticas across Spain's Mediterranean coast and the Balearics.
4. **Integrated boatyard plus dry storage.** Full-service yards combining haul-out, refit, and long-term storage with indoor and outdoor space and heavy-lift capability. Example: Plymouth Yacht Haven / Yacht Haven Quay with dry stack plus yard storage to 45 ft and 75-ton lift capacity.

### Port Adhoc Group

The Port Adhoc Group is a leading European marina manager and the French leader in "dry ports" (dry-stack marinas). It operates a network of 12+ dry-stack marinas across France, the Netherlands, and Sweden, with combined dry storage capacity exceeding 4,000 places. Storage types are indoor boat sheds and racked buildings plus hardstand cradle storage at some sites; target vessels are sports boats, cruisers, and sailing yachts typically up to 10–12 m. Services include launch and retrieval, wintering, maintenance, provisioning, and in some locations catering and accommodation. Port Adhoc is the closest European analogue to a multi-state dry-stack chain, comparable in concept to large South Florida operators with multiple facilities.

### Premier Marinas and Yacht Havens (UK)

Premier Marinas operates additional dry-stack facilities at Noss on Dart, Universal Marina (Hamble), and other south-coast locations, collectively offering several hundred dry-stack places. The Yacht Havens group is family-owned with nine marinas and boatyards in the UK and northern Europe. These sites are smaller than Trafalgar Wharf but form an important regional network along the UK's south coast.
