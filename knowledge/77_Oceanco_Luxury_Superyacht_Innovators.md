## Introduction

Oceanco has established itself as a titan in the world of luxury superyacht construction, synonymous with innovation, craftsmanship, and exclusivity. Since its inception in 1987, the Netherlands-based company has redefined maritime artistry by building custom superyachts that blend cutting-edge technology with bespoke design. Specializing in vessels over 80 meters, Oceanco caters to an elite clientele, delivering yachts that are not merely modes of transport but floating masterpieces tailored to their owners’ visions. This exploration delves into the who, what, where, when, why, and how of Oceanco, including its financial backing, to uncover the forces behind its rise to prominence.

## Who: The Visionaries Behind Oceanco

Oceanco’s story begins with a group of South African investors led by Richard Hein, the company’s first CEO. Hein’s vision was to create a yacht-building enterprise that could compete on a global stage. His leadership laid the foundation for Oceanco’s early operations, which initially spanned two continents. The company’s evolution was shaped by subsequent owners and leaders who brought distinct perspectives:

- **Theodore Angelopoulos (2002–2010)**: A Greek steel and shipping magnate, Angelopoulos acquired Oceanco in 2002 and shifted its focus to the niche market of 80m+ megayachts. His strategic vision introduced the “Y Generation” series, which elevated Oceanco’s reputation for innovation and luxury.
- **Mohammed Al Barwani (2010–present)**: An Omani billionaire and founder of MB Holding, Al Barwani acquired Oceanco in 2010. His passion for the sea and business acumen in oil, gas, and maritime industries have driven Oceanco’s expansion and commitment to sustainability. Al Barwani’s ownership has solidified Oceanco’s position as a leader in custom yacht building.
- **Marcel Onkenhout**: As CEO since 2010, Onkenhout has been instrumental in Oceanco’s modern era. With over 25 years at the company, he has overseen the delivery of iconic yachts and championed sustainability and technological advancements.
- **Arie Van Andel**: The Chief Technology Officer, Van Andel has been pivotal in integrating eco-friendly technologies, such as hybrid propulsion systems, into Oceanco’s designs.

Oceanco employs approximately 250 in-house staff but collaborates with over 1,000 subcontractors, including designers like Terence Disdale, Nuvolari Lenard, and Espen Øino. These partnerships ensure that each yacht is a unique blend of engineering and artistry.

## What: The Art of Superyacht Construction

Oceanco specializes in crafting custom superyachts, typically ranging from 80 to 140 meters, designed to reflect the unique desires of their owners. Unlike mass-produced yachts, Oceanco’s vessels are bespoke, with every detail—from propulsion systems to interior finishes—tailored to perfection. The company’s portfolio includes some of the world’s most iconic superyachts:

- **Alfa Nero (2007)**: An 82-meter yacht featuring an infinity pool that converts into a dance floor or helipad, showcasing Oceanco’s innovative design.
- **Black Pearl (2018)**: A 106.7-meter sailing yacht, the largest DynaRig sailing yacht in the world, renowned for its eco-friendly systems and striking design.
- **Bravo Eugenia (2018)**: A 109-meter yacht with a fuel-efficient LIFE design, emphasizing sustainability without compromising luxury.
- **KAOS (2019)**: One of the largest yachts built in the Netherlands, exemplifying Oceanco’s ability to handle complex, high-volume projects.

Oceanco’s yachts are celebrated for their quality, from flawless paintwork to seamless window integration and intricate teak detailing. The company’s “Life Cycle Support” program ensures ongoing maintenance, preserving the yachts’ pedigree for decades. Oceanco also pioneers sustainable technologies, such as the Energy Transition Platform (ETP), which supports a path to zero-emission yachts.

## Where: A Global Operation Rooted in the Netherlands

Oceanco’s headquarters and primary shipyard are located in **Alblasserdam**, Netherlands, approximately 20 kilometers from Rotterdam. This strategic location leverages the Netherlands’ rich maritime heritage and skilled workforce. The Alblasserdam facility, expanded in 2010 with a covered drydock, can accommodate yachts up to 140 meters and allows simultaneous construction of multiple vessels.

The company also maintains a **design and sales office in Monaco**, a hub for the global yachting elite. This office facilitates close collaboration with clients and designers, ensuring that Oceanco remains attuned to market trends. In its early years, Oceanco built hulls and superstructures in **Durban, South Africa**, before consolidating operations in the Netherlands to streamline production and enhance quality control.

Oceanco’s yachts sail worldwide, from the Mediterranean to the Caribbean, serving a clientele that includes billionaires, royalty, and industry titans. The company’s global reach is further evidenced by its strategic partnerships, such as with BMT for Lateral Naval Architects, enhancing its engineering capabilities.

## When: A Timeline of Excellence

Oceanco’s journey is marked by key milestones that trace its transformation from a modest venture to a global leader:

- **1987**: Founded by Richard Hein and South African investors, Oceanco begins building yacht hulls in Durban, South Africa, with finishing in the Netherlands.
- **1993**: Delivery of the 50-meter ANEDIGMI (later THE WELLESLEY), Oceanco’s debut yacht, marking its entry into the superyacht market.
- **2002**: Theodore Angelopoulos acquires Oceanco, redirecting its focus to 80m+ megayachts and launching the Y Generation series.
- **2008–2010**: Expansion of the Alblasserdam shipyard with a new covered drydock, enabling construction of larger yachts up to 140 meters.
- **2010**: Mohammed Al Barwani acquires Oceanco, ushering in an era of custom builds and sustainability initiatives. Marcel Onkenhout is appointed CEO.
- **2014–2018**: Delivery of landmark yachts like Equanimity (91.5m), Aquijo (85m), Jubilee (110m), Black Pearl (106.7m), and Bravo Eugenia (109m), cementing Oceanco’s reputation for innovation.
- **2022**: Controversy over a 417-foot yacht for Jeff Bezos, which required dismantling a historic Rotterdam bridge, highlights Oceanco’s high-profile projects and public scrutiny.
- **2025**: Oceanco continues to lead with sustainable designs and new projects, including four yachts under construction, such as Y722 and Y726.

## Why: The Drive for Perfection

Oceanco’s mission is to “create, deliver, and support innovative and uniquely designed, high-quality large yachts that are perfect for their visionary owners.” This ethos drives the company to push boundaries in design, technology, and sustainability. Several factors explain why Oceanco pursues this path:

- **Exclusivity**: By focusing on custom yachts over 80 meters, Oceanco caters to an ultra-wealthy clientele seeking one-of-a-kind vessels. This niche market allows the company to prioritize quality over quantity, delivering approximately two yachts per year.
- **Innovation**: Oceanco thrives on tackling unprecedented challenges, such as building the first 300-foot PYC yacht (Equanimity) or the eco-conscious Black Pearl. Its willingness to collaborate with unconventional partners, like those from the space industry, sets it apart.
- **Sustainability**: As environmental concerns grow, Oceanco has embraced sustainable technologies, such as hybrid propulsion and methanol-compatible systems, to future-proof its yachts and appeal to a new generation of eco-conscious clients.
- **Client Experience**: Oceanco views the yacht-building process as a journey, working closely with owners over 3–4 years to realize their dreams. This client-centric approach fosters loyalty and repeat business.
- **Legacy**: Oceanco aims to leave a lasting mark on the maritime industry, not just through its yachts but by setting benchmarks for craftsmanship and sustainability. Its vision to be “the most exclusive builder of the world’s coolest yachts” reflects this ambition.

## How: The Craftsmanship and Collaboration

Oceanco’s ability to deliver maritime artistry stems from its meticulous process and collaborative ecosystem:

- **Design and Engineering**: Each yacht begins with the owner’s vision, translated into reality by Oceanco’s in-house team and world-renowned designers. Naval architects like Lateral Naval Architects ensure structural integrity, while designers like Nuvolari Lenard create stunning interiors and exteriors.
- **Construction**: The Alblasserdam shipyard employs state-of-the-art facilities, including a covered drydock, to build yachts with precision. Subcontractors, part of the “Oceanco Co-maker Unlimited” initiative, bring specialized expertise, ensuring high standards.
- **Technology**: Oceanco integrates advanced systems, such as hybrid propulsion, battery storage for peak shaving, and the ETP for sustainable fuel transitions. These innovations enhance efficiency and reduce environmental impact.
- **Client Collaboration**: The 3–4-year build process involves constant dialogue with clients, from initial design to final delivery. Oceanco’s Monaco office facilitates this interaction, ensuring every detail aligns with the owner’s vision.
- **Life Cycle Support**: Post-delivery, Oceanco maintains yachts through a dedicated team, offering refits and upgrades to preserve their value and performance.
