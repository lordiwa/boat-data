### History of Online Boat and Yacht Sales

Online boat and yacht sales evolved from print classifieds in the 1990s to fully digital platforms today. Key milestones:
- **Early 1990s**: BoatTrader.com started as a print publication in 1991, targeting U.S. boaters with classified ads. YachtWorld.com emerged around the same time as a brokerage-focused site.
- **Mid-1990s to 2000s**: The internet boom led to sites like BoatTraderOnline.com (part of Trader Publishing) and early MLS (multiple listing services) for brokers. Print transitioned to web, with YachtWorld becoming a global database.
- **2010s**: Full digital shift—BoatTrader discontinued print in 2010. Companies like Boats Group (owning BoatTrader, YachtWorld, Boats.com) consolidated, adding features like price checkers and virtual tours. Social media and apps boosted visibility.
- **2020s**: Post-pandemic surge in used boat sales due to high interest rates hurting new sales. AI integration (e.g., matching buyers/sellers) and companies like Off The Hook Yachts (started 2012 as a side hustle, now $100M+ in annual buys) exemplify the "Carvana for boats" model. Traffic is steady at 16M+ monthly visits across major sites, with buyers saving 1.3M+ boats in searches recently.
- Overall trend: From in-person dealerships/boat shows to 70%+ of buyers starting online, driven by convenience and global access.
