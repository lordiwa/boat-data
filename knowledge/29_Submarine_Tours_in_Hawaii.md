- For non-swimmers: Most provide life vests/floats; opt for calm sites like protected bays.
- Eco focus: Tours emphasize no-touch rules to protect reefs (reef-safe sunscreen mandatory).
- From Quito (jet lag to UTC-10): Early tours help; pack light layers for boat rides.

### Top Recommendations by Island

**Maui** (Frequently ranked #1 for overall snorkeling—pristine reefs, turtles, and Molokini magic)
- **Molokini Crater + Turtle Town** (The ultimate combo): Boat to the crescent caldera for 100–200 ft visibility, then South Maui for honu sightings. Highly recommended operators:
  - **Redline Rafting** (from Kihei Boat Ramp): Fast raft (15-min ride), small groups, adventurous vibe. 2-hour Molokini-only (~$129) or 5-hour with South Maui (~$209). Award-winning, beats crowds. Great for excitement.
  - **Trilogy Excursions** (Ma'alaea Harbor): Premium catamaran, sailing focus, meals included. Often tops lists for luxury/comfort (~$200+).
  - **Pride of Maui** (Ma'alaea): Large catamaran, all-inclusive (food, alcohol), family-friendly. Molokini + Turtle Town (~$150–$200). High ratings for smooth rides.
  - **Maui Magic Snorkel** or **Maui Snorkeling Tours**: Smaller boats, multiple stops (Molokini, Coral Gardens, Turtle Town). ~$150–$200, personalized feel.
  - **Aqua Adventures** or **Calypso**: Snuba option (hybrid snorkel/scuba), good for variety (~$160+).
- Why top-rated: Recent reviews praise clear water, turtles, and crew knowledge. Molokini often sells out—book direct or via Viator.

**Big Island (Kailua-Kona area)** (Best for natural reefs, history, and iconic manta rays)
- **Kealakekua Bay / Captain Cook Monument** (Protected marine sanctuary, vibrant corals, fish, dolphins): Historic site with excellent snorkeling.
  - **Captain Cook Snorkeling Tours** or **Kona Snorkel Trips**: Small-group, efficient to bay, max water time. Highly rated as top-reviewed in Hawaii (~$100–$150). Often bundled with manta option.
  - **Fair Wind Cruises** or **Sea Quest Hawaii**: Catamaran options, family-oriented, meals included.
- **Manta Ray Night Snorkel** (Bucket-list: Float on surface as mantas glide below lights feeding on plankton—ethereal!):
  - **Sea Paradise**, **Kona Snorkel Trips**, or **Sea Quest Hawaii**: Small groups, "manta guarantee" policies (re-ride if no sightings), ~$135–$150. Evening tours (sunset start), magical and relaxing.
- Why standout: Kona's clear waters and protected spots make it a favorite; night mantas often called "once-in-a-lifetime."

**Oahu** (Most accessible from Honolulu/Waikiki—great for beginners/families)
- **Turtle Canyon / Waikiki Area** (Turtle hotspots with boat access):
  - **Living Ocean Tours**: Top-rated, most-reviewed on Oahu. Turtle-focused excursions, waterslides on some boats, small groups. Signature Turtle Canyons tour highly praised.
- **Hanauma Bay Nature Preserve** (Iconic protected crater bay—shallow reefs, tons of fish):
  - **Hanauma Bay Tours** or similar: Round-trip transport from Waikiki + gear (~$50–$100). Bay entry ~$25 (reservations required); guided options ease logistics.
- **Other gems**: Shark's Cove (North Shore, summer best) or Ko Olina Lagoons (calm, man-made).
- Why convenient: Short boat rides, easy add-on to city stays.

**Kauai** (Wilder, seasonal—summer north shore best)
- Poipu Beach or Tunnels (Makua)/Haena: Shore access or small tours for turtles/fish. Fewer large boats; focus on calm south shore in winter.
