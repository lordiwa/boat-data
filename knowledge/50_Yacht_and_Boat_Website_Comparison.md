### Overview of YachtWorld.com and BoatTrader.com

YachtWorld.com is a leading global platform primarily focused on buying and selling boats and yachts. It boasts the largest photo and video database of listings worldwide, with advanced search tools allowing filters by boat type, size, location, price, and more. Key features include search alerts for new listings, a marine directory connecting users to brokers, dealers, and manufacturers, yacht financing options, and resources like marine surveyors and shipping services. The site targets boat buyers, sellers, brokers, and marine professionals, offering content such as detailed listings (e.g., power boats like motor yachts and sport fishing vessels, sailboats like catamarans), articles on topics like boat storage and trends, and research on luxury yachts and manufacturers. It's part of a larger network under Boats Group, emphasizing comprehensive yacht data through listings and market insights.

BoatTrader.com positions itself as the top marketplace for buying and selling boats in the US, with a strong emphasis on location-based searches. It features extensive listings with photos, videos, prices, and direct seller contacts, plus search tools for filtering by boat type (e.g., pontoon, fishing, sailboats), make (e.g., Yamaha, Sea Ray), and location (e.g., by state like Florida or Texas). Other highlights include featured new boat brands, dealer directories organized by state, and articles/reviews on specific models like bass boats or jet skis. The audience includes individual buyers/sellers, dealers, and marinas, with content focused on practical data like boat prices (ranging from under $40,000 to high-end models), years, and types. It provides resources for transactions but is more US-centric compared to global sites.

Both sites compete in the boat/yacht marketplace space, offering data-driven listings (specs, prices, locations) alongside tools for buyers and sellers. YachtWorld has a broader international scope and deeper yacht-specific resources, while BoatTrader excels in US-focused, user-friendly searches for a wider range of boats.
