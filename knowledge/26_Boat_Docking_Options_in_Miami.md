### Overview of Boat Docking Options in Miami

Miami offers a wide range of docking facilities for boats, including public marinas managed by the City of Miami or Miami-Dade County, private yacht clubs, and commercial marinas. Options include wet slips (docked in water), dry storage (racked on land), moorings (anchored offshore with shuttle access), and transient dockage for short stays. Many provide amenities like fuel, pump-outs, restrooms, laundry, parking, and access to dining or charters. Rates vary by vessel size, duration (transient vs. long-term), and liveaboard status. Always contact the marina directly for current availability, as fees can change and reservations are often required. Key factors to consider: boat length (LOA), beam, draft, and whether you need power/water hookups.

Public marinas tend to be more affordable, while private ones offer premium services for larger yachts. Popular areas include Downtown Miami, Coconut Grove, Miami Beach, Key Biscayne/Virginia Key, and South Miami/Homestead. For superyachts (over 100'), look at facilities like Miami Beach Marina or Bayshore Landing.

### Tips for Docking in Miami
- **Reservations**: Book in advance, especially during peak season (winter) or events like Miami Boat Show.
- **Regulations**: Check for hurricane preparedness; some require evacuation plans. Follow environmental rules (no discharge in bays).
- **Alternatives**: If marinas are full, consider nearby areas like Fort Lauderdale or the Florida Keys.
- **Costs**: Expect additional fees for electricity (metered), water, liveaboard surcharges, and taxes (Miami-Dade adds 7% sales tax).
- **Accessibility**: Most are near airports (MIA) and highways; use apps like Dockwa for bookings.
